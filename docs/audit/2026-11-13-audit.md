# Project Valley visual and feel audit (2026-11-13)

Baseline: commit `70652d4` (main). Method: `npm run dev`, Playwright driving real Chrome (WebGPU, RTX 4090, 1280×720), fixed camera spots (spawn, range, build pad, animation stand, track, pen, night, dusk), inventory, pause and debug panel. Console read on every run.
"Before" screenshots: `docs/audit/screenshots/before/` (contact sheet `before_sheet.png`). "After" screenshots: `docs/audit/screenshots/after/`. Script: `tools/audit/shots.mjs`.

**Console:** zero errors. Only the two known warnings (`powerPreference` ignored on Windows; AudioContext waits for a click).
**Performance (baseline):** 60 fps vsync-capped in every scene on this PC, 0 hitches, 60-73 draw calls, 200-330k triangles. There is a lot of headroom. The game looks bad because of what it draws, not because it is slow.

## What looks and feels bad, ranked by impact

### 1. The whole world is flat-coloured (biggest problem)
Every surface is a single palette swatch with a vertical gradient. There is no surface detail at all: the ground is one smooth green, the concrete pads and walls are smooth grey, the range is a smooth brown slab. At any distance it reads as a blockout, not a game. Fixed: world-space detail from AI-generated textures (see progress log).

### 2. No post-processing look
The render chain was scene → tonemap → FXAA. No bloom (the HDR sun disc and lamps had nothing to bloom into), no vignette, no colour grade, no grain. Colours looked washed and the frame had no mood. Fixed: bloom, vignette, split-tone grade, grain, and a hurt-pulse hook.

### 3. Empty, hard-edged sky
Plain gradient dome. No clouds at all, so every scene has a dead blue/grey upper half. Fixed: animated cloud layer, sun-side lit.

### 4. Ground is bare; nothing grows
No grass, no rocks, no clutter anywhere inside the walls. The yard is a lawn-coloured plane. Fixed: ~20k wind-swayed grass tufts and 90 rocks (instanced, shrink with distance).

### 5. Few real models, props look generic
About 40 procedural Blender models in one palette: fine as a kit but chunky and repetitive (barrels, crates, cones, blocks). There are no vehicles other than the pickup, no wrecks, shelters, generators or dumpsters, so the yard feels like a test range. Partly fixed: new AI-generated props (TRELLIS2) where they landed; see progress log.

### 6. Characters
- Survivor and 4 zombie variants are about 1.9k triangles, proportions OK, but faces are blank and clothing is flat colour. Animation quality is good (mocap-retargeted, 26 clips, blend spaces), so this is a modelling/texture problem, not an animation problem.
- The user's complaint "no rigging or animation" does not match the code: rigs and clips exist and work (animation stand, zombies crowd GPU-skinned). What reads as missing is surface detail and variety.
- Not fixable overnight with the installed tools: no auto-rigger is built (UniRig not installed), so AI-generated characters cannot be animated. Left as a ranked item with options.

### 7. Lighting
Sun and hemisphere only. Ambient is strong, so shadows are weak and forms are flat. Night is a flat dark blue with very small lamp pools. Dusk is the best-looking state. Improved indirectly (grade + contrast); a proper AO pass is the next step.

### 8. HUD and UI
- HUD is minimal and legible: bars at the bottom-left, a time chip, "click to capture mouse". It works but is plain; no damage feedback on screen edges (hook added in post: `post.look.hurt`).
- Title, pause and settings menus are already well designed (condensed type, accent colour, transitions). No key art behind the title.
- Inventory is functional and dense but visually dark-flat; item icons are Blender renders and fine.

### 9. Game feel
Already strong in code: trauma-based camera shake, FOV kick, muzzle flash variants, tracers, casings, per-surface impacts and decals, melee trails, gore settings, hit markers. Gaps seen: no screen-edge hurt feedback (now hooked), no hit-flash on zombies, no dust/footstep puffs.

### 10. Audio
137 CC0 clips plus music/ambience layers. Everything is generic stock. New local-model SFX (stable-audio3-sfx) could fill gaps; not attempted overnight (see log).

### 11. Bugs and rough edges seen
- Third-person camera can start pitched up after teleport + look in the test harness (harness only, not reproduced in play).
- The pen/zombie shots in the script showed no zombies: `spawnInPen` spawns inside the pen, not at the teleport target (harness assumption, not a game bug).
- Known open items from M1/M2 QA still stand (FP rifle framing, repair prompt priority). Not re-triaged.
