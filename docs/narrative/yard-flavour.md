# The Yard: flavour notes and posters

The Yard was the Washington National Guard's vehicle and range lot outside Fennick. The Guard left in Month 3. Survivors now use it to test gear, settle bets and teach newcomers which end of the rifle goes where. Each piece is under 80 words. Ids are for when these move into `src/content/text/notes.ts`.

---

### yard_note_rules — painted board at the spawn gate
YARD RULES
1. Range is cold unless the flag is up.
2. Don't feed the pen. Don't name the pen.
3. Whatever you build on the pad, you take down.
4. Speed limit on the track is "whatever you can stop from".
5. Loot crates are for practice. Real loot is outside the wall.
6. If the bell rings, nobody is joking.
   — Sgt. D. Ruiz (ret.), management

### yard_note_range — poster on the range shed
GUARD MARKSMANSHIP: BREATHE, RELAX, AIM, SQUEEZE
(Original Guard poster, sun-faded. Below it in marker:)
Breathe — optional, but recommended.
Relax — not going to happen.
Aim — the head. Chest shots just make them annoyed.
Squeeze — then count what's left in the mag. Every time.
Brass goes in the bucket. The Ledger pays by weight.

To whoever built the "fortress" on Sunday:

Leave a door. Put the door where you can watch it.
